## The challenge

How people find and judge brands is changing on two fronts at once.

The first is AI search. Buyers increasingly ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Perplexity instead of searching. Brands had no reliable way to see whether they show up in those answers, how they compare with competitors, or which sources the models trust. A new category of AI visibility tools appeared quickly, but most stop at a score.

The second is media signal overload. Communications teams track earned coverage, social conversation, analyst commentary and competitor activity across dozens of tools and feeds. Traditional media analytics report volume after the fact. They rarely explain which narratives are forming, who is driving them, or what to do about it.

Highwire wanted proprietary answers to both problems: products clients would pay for, and data that makes the agency's own strategy sharper.

## AcroAnswers: from AI Index to a production product

**The MVP.** AcroAnswers started life as **AI Index**, announced in November 2025. The idea was a single, continuously updated score for a brand's presence in generative AI. It ran a set of unaided prompts (category questions with no brand named) and aided prompts (questions that name the brand), then applied weighted metrics to how often and how prominently the brand was mentioned in the answers. It proved there was demand, but a score on its own didn't tell a client what to do next.

**Architecting the platform.** I architected AcroAnswers, taking AI Index from that initial MVP vision to a production-ready product. The guiding idea was that a benchmark isn't a strategy. Clients needed to know why they were or weren't visible, and how to change it.

- **Multi-model tracking** across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity in a single dashboard.
- **A structured prompt framework.** More than 267 unique prompts per brand, split into branded and unbranded sets across the funnel: awareness, consideration, conversion, plus topical and reputational prompts.
- **Scale.** Around 30,000 AI responses analysed per brand every month, so trends reflect real patterns rather than one-off answers.
- **Competitive context.** Each brand's visibility is benchmarked against the competitors that matter to it.
- **Sources and citations.** The platform shows which websites and publications the models cite, pointing PR, content and SEO teams to the sources that shape AI answers.
- **From insight to action.** The output links visibility gaps to recommendations the account team can act on through earned, owned and content programmes.

**Going to market.** AcroAnswers launched publicly in September 2026 as a paid product, competing directly with third-party offerings from Profound, Meltwater, Semrush and Ahrefs. Its advantage is the agency around it. The same team that reads the data can run the programmes that move it.

## AcroIntelligence: from coverage database to intelligence engine

**Where it started.** AcroIntelligence began as an internal project to solve a basic problem: coverage data was scattered across tools, exports and spreadsheets. The goal was one central media and coverage database for every client.

**What it became.** Once the data lived in one place, it could do much more than report. AcroIntelligence became an intelligence engine, launched in June 2026 and described by Highwire as a proprietary Large Media Model.

- **Multiple data sources.** It ingests third-party APIs and feeds covering news, broadcast, podcasts and social, alongside analyst commentary and competitor activity.
- **Enrichment and tagging.** Every article is enriched with entities, key phrases, sentiment, topics and client-specific custom fields, turning raw coverage into structured, searchable data.
- **Narrative analysis.** Topic clustering, share of voice and trend comparisons show how narratives emerge, spread and shift over time.
- **Briefings and early warnings.** Custom industry and competitor briefings, plus alerts when the language, labels or competitors in a narrative start to change.
- **Plain-language access.** Integrated with AcroAI, Highwire's agentic AI platform, so teams can ask questions about competitors, coverage trends and messaging in plain English. Every answer is backed by citable articles.

**In use.** AcroIntelligence informs communications strategy for brands including Dropbox, NVIDIA and Dell. Account teams use it to benchmark announcements, find white space in crowded narratives, choose media targets, and back every recommendation with evidence from the coverage record.

## The results

- **AI Index MVP to production product.** AcroAnswers is now a revenue-generating platform that competes head-to-head with specialist AI visibility vendors.
- **More than 267 prompts and around 30,000 AI responses per brand each month**, across four major AI platforms.
- **A central, enriched media database** that grew from an internal project into a client-facing intelligence engine.
- **Strategy grounded in evidence** for brands including Dropbox, NVIDIA and Dell, with every insight traceable to the source coverage.
