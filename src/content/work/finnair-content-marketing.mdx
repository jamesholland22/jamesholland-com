## The challenge

Finnair has some of the most aggressive sustainability targets in the airline industry. It has to consider everything, from fuel to in-flight meals. Even the in-flight magazine counted: every printed copy added weight to every flight, and tons of paper each year.

Finnair wanted to move Blue Wings, its in-flight magazine, online. The new version had to do more than replace print. It needed to find new audiences in Finnair's key markets, earn traffic from search and social, and still be there for customers on board.

## The approach

We built Blue Wings around discoverability, route relevance and customer experience, not around the print issue cycle.

- **Organised by theme**, not by issue: destinations, lifestyle, sustainability, travel experience and the world of Finnair. Each story has lasting value for readers and a structure built for SEO.
- **Built for Finnair's routes and markets.** We published in English, Finnish, Japanese and Chinese, with SEO in each language, to reach new audiences in the markets Finnair flies to.
- **Integrated with social.** Stories were packaged for Finnair's social channels, including Instagram Stories, so they reached people beyond the website.
- **Discoverable everywhere.** Content is found through communities, search, social and the in-flight entertainment system.

## Pivoting through COVID-19

Then the pandemic hit. Finnair's capacity fell by 90%. Most of the fleet was grounded, routes changed week to week, and many people no longer trusted shared spaces, from public transport to aircraft cabins.

We pivoted the programme from inspiration to **reassurance and information**:

- **Content tied to routes and disruption**, so customers could find out what was flying, what had changed and what it meant for their trip.
- **Stories that showed the airline was still working.** We covered how Finnair kept cargo moving on empty passenger planes, maintained a grounded fleet, converted A330 cabins to carry medical supplies, and prepared to fly COVID-19 vaccines.
- **Myth-busting, with the experts.** We worked with Finnair's legal and safety teams to publish accurate, approved answers to travellers' biggest worries. One example is how cabin air is ventilated and filtered: it is completely changed every three to seven minutes and passes through HEPA filters similar to those in hospital operating theatres.

## The results

- **First-year traffic target beaten in eight weeks.** After a short planning cycle, Blue Wings passed its first-year audience goal within two months.
- **No more printed in-flight magazines**, saving tons of paper and gallons of fuel each year.
- **New markets and audiences**, with content in four languages built for search in each one.
- **A trusted source during disruption.** When travellers had questions about flying, Finnair had accurate, approved answers ready to share.
