## The challenge

Following a series of mergers and acquisitions, Ivanti rolled out a fresh brand identity and a playful new personality, centred on a single theme: *Powering the Everywhere Workplace*. A new name on the door wasn't enough — the brand needed mass-market awareness and deep audience recognition, fast. Highwire's job was to launch it with an activation engaging enough to make a B2B software brand part of the remote-work conversation.

## What I did

We developed and deployed a month-long social initiative across LinkedIn, Twitter, and Instagram, built around a simple, participatory idea. Alongside leading the strategy, I served as copywriter and scriptwriter for the campaign — from the video voiceover scripts to the social copy that carried the challenge week to week.

**The activation.** We launched the Work From Everywhere Challenge — a four-week competition that asked participants to share their most unusual remote-work setups and photos to win tech-focused prizes. Each week brought a new prompt, from working with a new perspective to working on the wild side.

**Creative content.** The campaign kicked off with a high-energy introductory video — a supercut built entirely from stock footage, scripted to pair a professional voiceover with the brand's playful new personality — augmented by Instagram polls, LinkedIn surveys, and interactive custom social cards.

**Community cultivation.** Rather than waiting for an audience, we went to where remote workers already gathered — seeding the campaign across dedicated Facebook groups, LinkedIn nomad networks, and targeted subreddits including r/vandwellers, r/RVLiving, and r/digitalnomad.

**The culmination.** The competition concluded with a wrap-up supercut celebrating the best user-generated submissions — the audience's own content becoming the brand's closing argument.

## The results

- **Turned passive observers into active participants**, establishing brand recognition for Ivanti's suite of new products
- **Set permanent organisational standards** for motion graphics, visual tone, and verbal identity across subsequent social campaigns
- **Built retargeting infrastructure** — capturing a newly engaged niche audience for ongoing campaigns
